### 4. Short Abstract (100 Words - For Form Summary)
Crises and sustainable resources never exist in silos: an industrial chemical release threatens downwind agricultural soil, urban air quality, and emergency trauma centers simultaneously. Existing municipal and agronomy tools operate in fragmented, expensive silos.

INNOVEX OS solves this by integrating all 6 hackathon domains into a unified, edge-computing operating system. Featuring phone-camera leaf pathology AI, Karnataka APMC Mandi price arbitrage, community blood donor geo-matching, clinical snakebite antivenom dosing, and real-time drone swarm telemetry, INNOVEX OS delivers immediate life-saving and economic impact.

---

### 5. Detailed Problem Statement (250 Words)
1. **Critical Medical Lag in the Golden Hour:** In tier-2/3 cities such as Davangere, road ambulances take 40+ minutes through urban traffic to deliver critical blood units or antivenom to rural Primary Health Centres (PHCs).
2. **Agricultural Economic Vulnerability:** Smallholder farmers lack real-time access to leaf pathology diagnostics and transparent mandi prices, frequently misidentifying fungal blights, overspending on chemical pesticides, and falling prey to local middlemen price exploitation.
3. **Disjointed Emergency Systems:** When industrial facilities suffer toxic ammonia leaks or boiler pressure surges, there is no autonomous bridge to alert municipal traffic corridors, downwind agricultural canals, or hospital trauma centers.
4. **Prohibitive Infrastructure Costs:** Commercial SCADA suites and drone fleet management software require costly monthly cloud subscriptions, preventing adoption by local Gram Panchayats and municipalities.

---

### 6. The Proposed Solution & Innovation
INNOVEX OS unifies all 6 domains into an autonomous digital twin:
* **AgriTech Deep Hub:** In-browser Computer Vision leaf diagnosis (Early Blight, Mildew), Subhash Palekar 100% organic Jeevamrutha formulation calculator, and Karnataka APMC Mandi price arbitrage (Davangere vs Shimoga vs Hubli).
* **HealthTech for Society:** Community Blood Donor geo-locator, Karnataka Big-Four Snakebite Antivenom (ASV) dosage clinical protocol, live 60FPS ECG cardiac rhythm monitor, citizen SOS panic beacon, and CPR 110-BPM metronome coach.
* **Robotics & Drones:** OpenStreetMap GPS waypoint routing, FLIR thermal infrared vision mode, Haversine flight distance math, collision avoidance, and Return-to-Launch (RTL).
* **Smart City:** Real-time Davangere weather and European AQI queried live from Open-Meteo satellites, coupled with emergency traffic green corridor preemption and Civic Scam Sentinel.
* **Industrial IoT:** SCADA boiler pressure telemetry, vibration FFT spectrum analysis, predictive bearing Remaining Useful Life (RUL), and automated toxic gas shutoff.
* **Open Innovation:** Cross-domain crisis drill orchestrator, Trilingual Voice AI Copilot (English, Hindi, Kannada), and citizen geo-hazard reporting portal.

---

### 7. Technology Stack Breakdown
* **Frontend:** HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Leaflet.js, OpenStreetMap, HTML5 Canvas.
* **Edge AI:** Client-side RGB pixel histogram processing running inside phone browsers (zero cloud GPU cost).
* **Live Telemetry:** Open-Meteo REST API (public meteorological satellites).
* **Voice & Audio:** Web Speech API (`SpeechSynthesis` & `webkitSpeechRecognition`).
* **Packaging & Cloud:** Progressive Web App (PWA) with offline Service Worker + Render Web Service ready.
